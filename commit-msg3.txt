ci: use runner's google-chrome instead of playwright chromium; probe timeout hint

The ubuntu smoke job pinned OVS_BROWSER to a playwright-installed
chromium. That build's headless probe (--dump-dom + --virtual-time-
budget over a file:// page) hangs indefinitely (reproduced locally
against chromium-1234: even with --timeout and old --headless it never
completes), and its sandbox crashes on ubuntu-24.04 runners. The
runner image ships Google Chrome, which completes the same probe in
<1s, so the job now uses it and drops the playwright download
(also makes CI faster).

Also:
- render-html.py: catch the probe TimeoutExpired and explain the
  playwright-chromium incompatibility with a remediation hint (use
  Chrome/Edge/Chromium or playwright's chrome-headless-shell) instead
  of a bare 60s timeout error.
- chrome_common.py: drop the duplicate --no-sandbox in the
  OVS_NO_SANDBOX fast path (flags() already appends it).
- README: document that playwright's main chromium build must not be
  used for headless rendering.
